Who is Coach Arpak?

Champion. Competitor.
Chess Coach.

Arpak Worya Mohammed Qanee has won the BC Senior Chess Championship five times — in 2018, 2019, 2022, 2023 and 2026 — and reached a peak FIDE rating of 2296 with the title of Candidate Master. His competitive career spans events in Iraq, Qatar, Yemen and Canada.

Born in Erbil, in the Kurdistan region of Iraq, Arpak grew up in a family of chess players and has spent decades at the board, where he became known for his strategic mindset and competitive spirit.

As a coach, he teaches what tournament play taught him: how to evaluate positions, make better decisions and learn from mistakes. His focus is on helping juniors and teens build real chess thinking — whether they are just getting serious or already competing.
