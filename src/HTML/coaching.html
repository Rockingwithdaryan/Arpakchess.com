Coaching

Master the thinking behind the move.

Coaching grounded in decades of real competition — teaching players how to evaluate positions, recognize consequences and make stronger decisions at the board.

Formats

Two ways to train

1:1 Coaching

Personal sessions built around one player’s games, strengths and gaps. Ideal for focused improvement, reviewing tournament games and preparing for upcoming events.

Group Coaching

Small-group sessions with shared positions, guided practice games and the challenge of training alongside other motivated players.

What Coach Arpak Teaches

Inside a session

  • Position evaluation Reading a position the way a competitive player does — what matters and why.
  • Calculation & tactics Finding stronger moves and spotting what the opponent is threatening.
  • Decision-making Choosing a plan, weighing consequences and playing with purpose.
  • Endgames Converting advantages and holding difficult positions.
  • Game review Going through the player’s own games to stop repeat mistakes.
  • Tournament preparation Practical habits, time management and composure for competition.

Each session is shaped around the player’s level and goals.
