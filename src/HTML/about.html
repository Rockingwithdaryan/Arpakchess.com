About Coach Arpak

From Erbil to Vancouver

Early Life & Introduction to Chess

Arpak Worya Mohammed Qanee was born in Erbil, in the Kurdistan region of Iraq, into a family of chess players, and discovered the game at a very young age. From his first games, he showed a natural affinity for chess. He would climb onto the roof of his house to practise alone, and he regularly played against his family members, who were avid players themselves.

Rise Through the Ranks

Arpak quickly built a reputation as a prodigy, and his talent and determination were soon recognized across the country. He went on to win countless tournaments, earning the respect of peers and mentors alike, and eventually became Iraq’s number one chess player. His career took him to international events in Iraq, Qatar and Yemen, and later to Canada, where he has won the BC Senior Chess Championship five times.

Arpak also trained his own son and daughter, and both became strong players. His son Daryan Worya, guided by his father’s coaching and mentorship, became Iraq’s number one youth chess player at just eight years old.

Chess & Character

Away from competition, Arpak is a dedicated family man with a genuine passion for mentoring younger players. He believes chess is as much about character as it is about strategy: it teaches patience, discipline, confidence, resilience and good sportsmanship — lessons that carry well beyond the board.

In the Community

Arpak is helping start a student chess club at King George Secondary in Vancouver, with the school’s permission. The club is for the students first: a place to play, learn and grow local chess culture for the next generation.
